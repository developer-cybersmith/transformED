Key Caveats and Gotchas

  • No credit rollover. Unused monthly credits expire at end of each billing cycle across all plans.
  • Commercial use ambiguity on Free. The plan card says "commercial use" but the ToS restricts free accounts to personal, non-commercial use. Treat free tier as non-commercial to avoid account action.
  • API billing is separate. Subscription credits fund the web studio only. API calls are billed per-byte, metered separately, and require a prepaid balance.
  • CJK language surcharge. One million Chinese/Japanese/Korean characters costs $30–$45 via API (not $15) because each CJK character encodes as 3 UTF-8 bytes.
  • Self-serve plans allow content to be used for AI training. Fish Audio's terms permit usage data and generated content to support model development on Free, Plus, Pro, and Max. Zero data retention is Enterprise-only.
  • Team seat gaps. Pro caps at 3 seats, Max at 10. No published per-seat add-on pricing for additional members beyond those caps.
  • Fair-use ceiling on S2.1 Pro Free API tier is not published; use at your own risk for production workloads.
  • Account termination destroys cloned voices. Fish Audio does not guarantee export of voice models on account suspension — keep your original recordings elsewhere.
