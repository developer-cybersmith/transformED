TTS Latency Benchmarks 2026 Research Report · Sep 2026

Time-to-First-Audio (TTFA), streaming latency, and generation speed across Fish Audio, ElevenLabs, Azure TTS, Sarvam AI, and OpenAI TTS — with source attribution and methodology notes for every claim.

Key finding: Vendor-published TTFA numbers are measured from the provider's nearest region under ideal load. Independent benchmarks (Coval, Gradium, forasoft) consistently report real-world numbers 50–150ms higher. Metrics labelled "(vendor)" below are self-reported; those labelled "(Coval)" or "(independent)" reflect third-party testing.

Cross-Source Methodology Notes

  • Coval (benchmarks.coval.ai/tts): Independent voice AI evaluation platform. 750–1,471 runs per model. WebSocket APIs for all providers except OpenAI (HTTP). Captures P25, P50, P75, IQR. Refreshes continuously. Data snapshot: May 4, 2026. Most credible source — largest sample sizes.
  • Gradium self-benchmark (March 2026): Paris-based. WebSocket multiplexing test. Self-published P50 258ms standard / 214ms with multiplexing. Gradium also ranked #1 in the independent Coval test (155ms) — treat self-published numbers as directionally consistent but optimistic.
  • forasoft / FutureAGI / texttolab (May 2026): Editorial reviews "verified against official documentation." No methodology detail for how TTFA was measured. Figures for Azure, OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, and Fish Audio from these sources should be treated as estimates ±50ms.
  • GrowwStacks Sarvam test: Production integration. "Raw, unoptimised API calls, no caching." Hindi-English conversations. Measures actual audio start delay, not TTFB. Most realistic production estimate for Sarvam AI.
  • Sarvam vendor documentation: TTFB <200ms measured from same-region India infrastructure. TTFB ≠ TTFA (does not include audio decoding / playback start). 8kHz telephony test, not high-fidelity narration.
  • Key distinction (forasoft): "The first byte is often just a file header. Measure time-to-first-audio, not time-to-first-byte." TTFB and TTFA differ by 50–150ms in practice.
  • Universal caveat (FutureAGI): "Provider-published TTFA numbers come from their nearest region under ideal load. Your number from your egress point at your peak traffic shape will be 50–150ms higher."
