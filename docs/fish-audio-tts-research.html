1. Executive Summary

Fish Audio's paid API costs $15.00 / million UTF-8 bytes — equivalent to roughly 12 hours of English speech or 180,000 words. A free production API tier (s2.1-pro-free) existed with no hard character cap through 31 August 2026 (its extended deadline); its post-August status is unconfirmed as of the research date (2026-09-03).

Latency for the WebSocket streaming endpoint is self-reported as sub-500 ms end-to-end in normal conditions, with a time-to-first-byte of 180–220 ms in light-load development environments. No SLA is published.

Fish Audio is positioned as an 11× cheaper alternative to ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 ($15 vs $165/1M chars) and 4× cheaper than ElevenLabs Flash ($15 vs $60/1M chars).

6. Streaming Architecture & Latency

WebSocket endpoint

wss://api.fish.audio/v1/tts/live

  • Serialization: MessagePack
  • Auth: Bearer token in Authorization header
  • Bidirectional streaming

Audio formats

  • WAV, PCM
  • MP3 (64, 128, 192 kbps)
  • Opus (24, 32, 48, 64 kbps or auto)
  • Default sample rate: 44,100 Hz (48,000 Hz for Opus)

Latency figures (self-reported by Fish Audio)

No independent third-party benchmark exists. All figures below are from Fish Audio's own blog or documentation. No SLA is published. Self-reported only

Latency controls in the API

Three latency modes are available per request:

  • low — fastest response, potentially lower quality
  • balanced — reduced latency with acceptable quality
  • normal — best quality (default)

Buffer controls

  • chunk_length: 100–300 characters (default 300)
  • min_chunk_length: 0–100 characters (default 50)
  • FlushEvent: forces immediate synthesis of buffered text for low-latency interactive use
  • condition_on_previous_chunks: enabled by default for voice consistency across chunks
Streaming benefit: A response requiring 6–8 seconds of total generation time can start delivering audio to the client in 80–150 ms when using WebSocket streaming, because audio chunks are sent before the full generation is complete.

8. Unverified & Flagged Claims

Flag 1 — Free tier post-August 2026 status Unverified

The s2.1-pro-free tier was confirmed active through 31 August 2026. Whether it has been extended, ended, or made permanent after that date is unknown as of 2026-09-03. The research date is 3 days past the stated deadline. Verify at fish.audio before using.

Flag 2 — Promotional subscription prices Unverified exact values

Third-party sources cite Plus at $5.50/mo and Pro at $37.50/mo on annual billing. These may be promotional prices. The Plus list price is $20/mo and Pro is $150/mo. Verify directly at fish.audio/pricing.

Flag 3 — All latency figures are self-reported No independent benchmark

The TTFB figures (180–220 ms, <500 ms end-to-end) come from Fish Audio's own blog posts. No independent third-party benchmark was found. The article itself cautions that "development machine benchmarks do not predict production behavior" and results should be tested "from the region where your users are." Degradation to ~2,300 ms was observed at 200 concurrent users in an undisclosed test.

Flag 4 — Azure TTS and Sarvam AI pricing not retrieved Gap in research

Search results did not return authoritative 2026 pricing for Azure TTS or Sarvam AI Bulbul v2. Azure TTS pricing is available at azure.microsoft.com/pricing/details/cognitive-services/speech-services/. Sarvam AI pricing requires direct contact or checking sarvam.ai.

Flag 5 — "#1 in blind A/B tests" claim Unverified — source is Fish Audio marketing

One third-party source (texttolab.com) cites Fish Audio as ranking "#1 in blind A/B tests" and "wins 60% of blind tests." The methodology, sample size, and test conditions are not specified. Treat as marketing language, not independently verified quality benchmark.
